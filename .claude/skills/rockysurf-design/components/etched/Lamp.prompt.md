Replaces StatusBadge in the etched skin. Three arcs running, two dashed and pulsing while provisioning, none when stopped, a lens with the beam cut when failed or terminated. The five product meanings survive in the colour.

```jsx
<Lamp status="running" />
<Lamp status="provisioning" />
```
Do not use both Lamp and StatusBadge on one screen — two vocabularies for one fact.
