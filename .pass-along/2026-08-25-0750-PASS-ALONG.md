## Decisions and rules

- **`requiresRepos` asks, never demands** (#90/PR #91): empty list → confirm checkbox on the
  createAnyway doctrine (offered only after a submit raises it, reason on screen). Pack
  `setupScript`s must tolerate `$REPOS=''` — documented in writing-a-pack.md and the skill
  contract; gas-town (the only shipped reader) already does.
- **A supplied key ends as the only key** (#92/ADR-0008, amends ADR-0002 for this case only):
  RS's key is a provisioning tool, not a standing credential. The removal step is REQUIRED
  (failed guard fails the plan, both keys stay — no lockout path) and surgically removes one
  line, never rewrites `authorized_keys` (BYO hosts have keys Rocky Surf didn't put there).
  The host-key secret survives retirement — only the user-key half is retired.
- **The voice profile governs README/landing prose** (PR #97): no em dashes anywhere in the
  prose (the profile's most reliable marker), at most the load-bearing "X, not Y" verdicts,
  contractions. Owner-dictated pieces (hero, principles, BYO lead-ins) stay verbatim over
  profile tendencies — the owner's actual words outrank inferred style.
- **Region stays installation-level** unless the owner asks otherwise; a per-create region
  collides with the region-scoped offering/pricing design and would need an ADR-grade pass.
